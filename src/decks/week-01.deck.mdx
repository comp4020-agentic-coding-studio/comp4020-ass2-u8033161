# Sustainability language on a box is mostly marketing.

What actually decides how it's built: a shipping-cost formula and a
return/liability calculation.

---

## Three boxes, three logics

1. Apple — brand experience
2. lululemon / Patagonia — logistics cost
3. OnTheList — regulatory compliance

None of the three needed an appeal to the environment.

---

## 1. Apple

A layered reveal. Accessories hidden behind a second panel, found by touch.
Every material and colour agrees with every other one.

**None of this is required to get a phone to your door intact.**

It's required to make opening the box feel like part of buying the phone.

---

## 2. lululemon, Patagonia

A pair of shoes ships in a box several sizes too big.

Not generosity — a retailer shipping thousands of SKUs a day saves real
money stocking three or four standard box sizes instead of one per product.

The extra cardboard and air freight is the price of that standardisation.

---

## 3. OnTheList

A tear-strip moulded into the box. An "Aviation Security" dangerous-goods
label on the side.

Neither is about you. Both exist because a regulator requires them before
a flammable liquid goes on a plane.
