## Before the lab

Bring back your week 1 package, plus whatever notes you have from weeks 2
and 4 on its material and cubic-weight cost.

## In the lab

- **Pick a concept** — from Monday's lecture, choose one choice-
  architecture idea (default path, salience, framing, sequencing) to apply.
- **Redesign the reveal** — sketch or describe a change to your package's
  opening sequence that uses that concept. The product, its price and its
  ISTA-relevant protection (week 3) must not change — only the packaging
  and the order in which things become visible or accessible.
- **Cost it** — using week 2's material logic or week 4's cubic-weight
  formula, estimate whether your redesign is roughly cost-neutral, or adds
  a real cost. If it adds cost, say where.
- **Defend it** — in pairs, explain why your redesign is worth (or isn't
  worth) that cost, given what it's trying to do to perceived value.

## Afterwards

Keep your sketch. Week 10 asks a related but different question about the
same unboxing moment: not what it makes someone feel, but who can
physically manage it at all.
