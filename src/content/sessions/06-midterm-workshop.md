## Before the lab

Bring your notes and numbers from weeks 2, 4 and 5 — flute identification,
cubic-weight calculation, sustainability-claim critique — whichever you
completed for your own week 1 package.

## In the lab

- **Draft** — outline a diagnosis for your own package (or one of Apple,
  lululemon, OnTheList): which of the three logics is it optimising for,
  and which specific number or standard from weeks 2-5 is your evidence?
- **Peer review** — swap outlines with another pair. Your job is not to
  agree or disagree with their conclusion, but to check whether the
  evidence they cited actually supports it, or just gestures at it.
- **Gap list** — for your own case, list which weeks' frameworks you
  haven't actually applied yet (only described in words), and commit to
  which one you'll run the real numbers on before Assignment 1 is due.

## Afterwards

This lab is direct preparation for Assignment 1, the midterm diagnostic —
see the [Assessments](/assessments/) page for what's expected and when
it's due.
