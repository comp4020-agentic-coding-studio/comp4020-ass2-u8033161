## Before the lab

Come with a product already chosen, and the packaging it currently ships
in (a photo is fine if you don't have the physical item).

## In the lab

- **Justify the choice** — state why your chosen product's current
  packaging is a genuine redesign opportunity: what's wrong with it, or
  what trade-off it's currently making that you think is wrong.
- **Work the six constraints** — for each of flute/board, cubic weight,
  sustainability-claim honesty, returns economics, compliance, and
  accessibility, state what your redesign does. "Unchanged, because—" is a
  valid answer if you can justify it; "I haven't thought about that one
  yet" is not.
- **Name your hardest trade-off** — identify the one pair of constraints
  that pull against each other hardest in your redesign, and practise
  defending your choice out loud with a partner.
- **Get feedback** — swap with another pair. Their job is to find a
  seventh constraint (from any earlier week) your redesign hasn't
  considered, if one exists.

## Afterwards

This studio session is direct preparation for the final project. See the
[Assessments](/assessments/) page for the full brief, and bring your work
to week 12's defense.
