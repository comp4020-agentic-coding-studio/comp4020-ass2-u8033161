## Before the lab

Bring back your week 1 package, and look for any sustainability-adjacent
text or symbol on it before the lab starts: a recycling triangle, a
material code, a certification mark, a line of marketing copy about the
environment.

## In the lab

- **Find the claim** — if your package has no such claim, borrow a
  classmate's, or use whichever week 1 case (Apple, lululemon, OnTheList)
  is closest to something you've seen elsewhere.
- **Find the publisher** — is the claim made by the brand itself, a
  third-party certifier, an industry body, or a government scheme? Look it
  up if you don't already know.
- **Apply the test** — using Monday's Fibre Box Association example as a
  template, separate two questions: is the underlying methodology or
  standard sound, and does the publisher have a direct interest in you
  believing the comparison it implies? A claim can pass one test and fail
  the other.
- **Name the gap** — write one sentence on what information you'd need
  (an independent audit, a competing material's own LCA, the raw study
  itself) to actually check the claim rather than just trust or distrust
  it on reputation.

## Afterwards

Keep your claim and your one-sentence gap. Week 6 asks you to place your
week 1 case study's sustainability posture, if it has one, against the
other four logics you now have numbers for.
