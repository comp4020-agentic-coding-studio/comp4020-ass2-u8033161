## Before the lab

Bring back your week 1 package one more time. If you don't still have it,
use a photo or a clear memory of its opening mechanism.

## In the lab

- **Audit** — go over your package looking specifically for physical
  opening barriers: tape that needs strong grip to start, a tab too small
  to find without close vision, print too small to read at a glance, an
  insert that needs two hands and fine motor control to remove. Name a
  specific group of people each barrier would actually exclude, not a
  vague "some people."
- **Fix one thing** — sketch a change that removes or reduces that
  barrier, using the OXO principle: a fix that helps the excluded group
  usually helps everyone else too.
- **Check compatibility** — does your fix still satisfy at least one
  earlier constraint you've already priced (this package's cubic-weight
  number from week 4, its ISTA-relevant protection from week 3, or a
  compliance requirement)? If your fix breaks one of those, say so and
  adjust it.
- **Cost it** — estimate, even roughly, whether the fix adds material
  cost, assembly time, or new tooling — and whether that cost looks small
  next to what it buys.

## Afterwards

Keep your fix. Week 11's redesign studio asks you to combine this
accessibility work with five other constraints, on a new product, for the
integrative assessment.
