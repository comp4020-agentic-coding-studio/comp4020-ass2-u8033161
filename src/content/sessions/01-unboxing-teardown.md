## Before the lab

Bring a package. Any online order from the last month with the outer box
still around — the emptier and more chewed-up the better, since the marks
of shipping are half of what we're reading.

## In the lab

- **First 10 minutes** — in pairs, apply the lecture's three-logic
  framework (brand experience / logistics cost / compliance) to your own
  package. Pick the logic that best explains the box, and find one physical
  feature that's your evidence for it.
- **Next 20 minutes** — swap packages with another pair. Argue for a
  *different* logic than the one they picked, using a feature they didn't
  mention. Most packages support more than one reading; the exercise is to
  notice that before you commit to one.
- **Last 15 minutes** — as a class, collect one open question per package:
  a claim about the box you can't settle just by looking at it. These
  become the running list the next few weeks answer.

## Afterwards

Keep your one-paragraph prediction. In week 4, once you have Australia
Post's actual cubic-weight formula, you'll re-run it on the same package and
check whether the logistics-cost story survives contact with the real
number.
