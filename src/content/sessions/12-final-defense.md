## Before the lab

Bring your finished redesign from week 11's studio: the product, the
packaging concept, and your notes on all six constraints.

## In the lab

- **Present** — a short presentation of your redesign: the product, what
  changed, and which of the six constraints from week 11 drove the
  biggest decisions.
- **Locate it** — state which of week 1's three logics (brand experience,
  logistics cost, compliance) your redesign leans on most, pointing to a
  specific physical feature as evidence, not a general description of the
  product category.
- **Defend a trade-off** — pick the one constraint you traded away or
  downweighted the most, and defend why that was the right call for this
  specific product, under questioning from peers.
- **Answer the throughline claim** — state, in one sentence, whether your
  own semester's work supports, complicates, or refutes the course's
  central claim that sustainability language is mostly marketing while
  cost and liability formulas do the real work — and name the single
  piece of evidence from the semester that most shaped your answer.
