Marisol designed this course and teaches the weekly lecture. Background is
supply-chain economics, with five years pricing freight contracts for an
Australian 3PL before moving into teaching — which is the source of the
shipping-cost formulas the course leans on from week 4 onward.

**Consultation:** no standing office hours; email to arrange a time.
Course-level questions (assessment weighting, extensions, the syllabus) go to
Marisol by email. Feedback on a specific piece of work goes to whoever marked
it, in the Lab where it's returned — that's a faster path than email and
means the marker's reasoning is fresh. If a Lab-level question genuinely
doesn't get resolved there, escalate to Marisol.
